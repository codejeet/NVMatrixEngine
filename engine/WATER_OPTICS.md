# Laser and water optics

The playable lab includes two collimated **1.5 W** lasers (one aimed through the prism, one into the tank), a **28 W** equal-energy white tank illuminator, and the original **40 W** prism collimator. **L** cycles 532 → 450 → 638 nm. Adjust the prism with the existing precision controls. The tank is on the right of the source; its ripples are a frozen optical snapshot, not a fluid simulation.

## Physical model and units

Source power is radiant watts; geometry is metres. Uniform white emission samples 380–780 nm with the matching wavelength PDF. Each monochromatic source uses its actual wavelength, without a white-spectrum PDF or RGB-brightness normalization. Every photon carries source watts divided by its source's sample count. Refraction changes ray direction and differential footprint, **not photon power by eta-squared**. Fresnel branching and Beer–Lambert extinction account for transmission, reflection, TIR and medium losses. Room/tank receivers use area-normalized photon splats; Lambertian shading divides irradiance by pi.

Water's refractive index is computed from **IAPWS R9-97 equation 1**, at 20 °C and 998.207 kg/m³, then divided by a constant ambient-air index of 1.00027. A 401-entry GPU lookup table avoids evaluating the dispersion formula inside ray loops. The CPU test verifies three independent published reference values, using their reference temperature/density. Attribution: [International Association for the Properties of Water and Steam, R9-97](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/Rindex).

Absorption comes from [Pope and Fry (1997), measured pure-water data](https://omlc.org/spectra/water/abs/pope97.html), using [OMLC's 2.5 nm table](https://omlc.org/spectra/water/data/pope97.txt). The source values are converted from **cm⁻¹ to m⁻¹**. Beyond 727.5 nm the table interpolates to [Hale and Querry (1973)](https://omlc.org/spectra/water/data/hale73.txt) at 750/775/800 nm. This sparse far-red extension is not claimed to have the accuracy of the visible measurements. Red light therefore attenuates much more strongly than blue light in the tank; no blue tint is painted onto the material.

Photometric conversion uses `683 × CIE1931_XYZ(lambda) × radiantPower`, followed by one explicit fixed exposure, `400 / (106.856895 × 683)`. Thus one internal Y-radiance unit corresponds to **182.458148 cd/m² before tone mapping**. This preserves the lab's existing equal-energy-white brightness while retaining wavelength-dependent luminous efficacy. It does not imply calibrated monitor luminance. See [NIST's photometric conversion](https://www.nist.gov/pml/sensor-science/optical-radiation/realization-candela) and [BIPM's candela definition](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/candela). Existing decorative neon, housing indicators and artistic fill lights are not a measured luminaire model.

## Real-time choices

- One fixed **65,536-photon total budget** by default: half for the prism collimator, one quarter for the tank illuminator and one eighth per laser. Disabled sources return their budget to active sources. All traversal remains in DXR ray-generation shaders; no compute traversal or new denoiser.
- An **8,960-triangle, watertight water boundary** is built into a static BLAS once. Analytic ripple normals and their derivatives drive Snell refraction and photon footprints. A separate 256² tank-floor chart covers 13.68 m²; the full five-chart atlas is 1280×256. Static water does not cause perpetual history invalidation or per-frame BLAS refits.
- Two GPU beam-raygen threads generate at most **16 deterministic Fresnel-split segments per laser**, with explicit truncation counters. Analytic ray/cylinder overlap and four bounded quadrature samples estimate visible single scattering. No per-pixel raymarch or emissive beam mesh is added.
- Air uses an explicitly chosen haze scattering coefficient of **0.012 m⁻¹**, HG asymmetry 0.25. Water adds **0.005 m⁻¹** isotropic scattering, representing a lightly scattering tank rather than a measured turbidity sample. These scattering coefficients are scene parameters, **not measured pure-water absorption data**. A side-visible beam needs scattering; `--no-haze` removes air scattering/extinction. The [normalized phase-function convention](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Volume_Scattering/Phase_Functions) is tested, including zero-scattering visibility and power linearity.
- Texture-space accumulation remains the existing bounded EMA; DLSS RR/SR remains the only screen-space reconstruction pass. Changing wavelength invalidates the optical atlas, not the global RR history. Camera/rolling/orbit interpolation is preserved.

## Accuracy boundaries

The photon caustics use wavelength-resolved geometrical optics. The camera retains its **550 nm reference IOR** and RGB absorption sampled at 610/550/460 nm; it is not a fully spectral camera. Beam visibility uses narrow, piecewise cylindrical envelopes, not a full photon-beam density estimator; strongly focused/defocused envelopes are approximate. Charted receiver spots come only from photons. Uncharted moving props receive an analytic terminal-beam spot approximation, preventing laser hits from going completely dark without inventing texture charts for every prop.

The model is unpolarized and incoherent: no diffraction, interference, speckle or polarization transport. Water is a prescribed optical volume, without animated waves, splashes, buoyancy or fluid dynamics. Visible in-scattering is evaluated for laser paths, not all broad/decorative sources; multiple volume scattering, general volumetric caustics and arbitrary emissive-source photon guiding are not implemented. The current camera path caps, photon-footprint caps, non-charted receiver losses and multilayer RR-guide limitations remain explicit. This is a physically based, bounded real-time implementation—not a metrology-grade or fully converged light simulation.

## Validation

`NVMatrixEngineWaterTest` checks IAPWS reference values, absorption units/interpolation, dispersion and manifold water geometry. `test-water.ps1` renders green/blue/red, flat water, disabled lasers/water/haze, and fixed-point accumulation controls. `validate-water.mjs` checks every captured guide for finiteness, raw photon energy accounting, water focusing versus a flat interface, spectral attenuation, disabled contributions and float/fixed parity.

Diagnostic switches: `--laser-nm=380..780`, `--flat-water`, `--no-water`, `--no-lasers`, `--no-haze`. The optical power ledger reports emitted, deposited, medium loss, escaped, unmapped/blocked, truncated and excluded original-collimator entrance-reflection power. It accounts for the sampled transport class, not all artistic scene illumination or a full multiple-scattering energy solution.
