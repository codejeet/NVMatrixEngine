NVMatrixEngine - Water Lab - research preview
https://github.com/codejeet/NVMatrixEngine

Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder. Open Play Water Lab.cmd.
Windows 11 x64 and a compatible high-end NVIDIA RTX GPU/driver are required.
Validated on RTX 5090; other hardware is not certified. No installer or admin
rights are needed. CUDA Toolkit is not needed to run the optional CUDA demo.

Esc: settings. WASD: roll. Space: jump. Right drag: look. Wheel: zoom.
Tab: first person. Ctrl: dive. E: interact / board nearby boat / exit boat.
Boat W/S: thrust, A/D: steer. T: wall inlet. I: inspect inlet.
P: pause water. Period: one step. B: reset water. U: HUD details. F8: frame gen.

The default launcher uses DX12 simulation, Balanced DLSS and automatic frame
generation where supported. Fisheye is optional in settings and pauses FG.
Change lighting, flashlight, ball sink/float, music volume and water quality
in Esc. Applying water quality resets the pool. Filling stops at capacity.
The glass ball starts in Sink mode; choose Float in Esc to use a hollow ball.

Optional launchers: CUDA water (uniform solver), larger deep pool, experimental
narrow-band CUDA ownership, and ReSTIR PT indirect lighting. These are not
promised speedups. Deep-pool and high particle-count modes are more demanding.

This unsigned preview may receive a Windows reputation warning. Obtain it only
from the official repository/release. Verify the release ZIP SHA-256 sidecar.
If startup fails, read NVMatrixEngine.log and lab-error.txt in this folder.
Do not copy the executable away from its shaders, assets, DLLs or D3D12 folder.

README.md has features and links. COPYRIGHT.md and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md describe
terms; bundled component licenses are in licenses/. No blanket 60 FPS guarantee.
Generated presentation frames are not extra simulation/rendered frames.
