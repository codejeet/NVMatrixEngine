NVMatrixEngine 0.1.5-preview - Water Labs, Ocean Island and Neon Night
https://github.com/codejeet/NVMatrixEngine

Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder. Choose a launcher:
  Play Water Lab.cmd             Small indoor lab, legacy DX12 water
  Play Large Water Lab.cmd       Larger indoor lab, Hamiltonian water
  Play Extra Large Water Lab.cmd Outdoor ocean and island, Hamiltonian water
  Play Neon Night.cmd            Rain-wet neon alley with imported PBR props

Windows 11 x64 and a compatible high-end NVIDIA RTX GPU/driver are required.
Developed on RTX 5090; other hardware is not certified. No installer, admin
rights or CUDA Toolkit are needed. This package uses the DX12 fluid backend.

Neon Night: WASD rolls the ball, Space jumps, right-drag looks, wheel zooms,
Tab switches view, R restarts, and Esc opens settings. Assets are bundled for offline use; see engine/NEON_NIGHT.md.

Water labs: Esc: settings. WASD: move. Space: jump / hold underwater to swim upward.
Right drag: look. Wheel: zoom. Tab: first person. Ctrl: dive.
E: interact / board nearby boat / exit boat. Boat W/S: thrust, A/D: steer.
T: water inlet. I: inspect inlet / ocean overview. Y: ocean day/night.
P: pause water. Period: one step. B: reset water. U: HUD details. F8: frame gen.

The ocean spans 256 x 256 metres with 6 m offshore water depth. It uses fixed
1 m fluid cells and a 2 m wave grid. Dynamic cell sizing around rigid bodies
has been removed. Broad swells use a 1.8 m height scale and a 48 m minimum
initial wavelength. Hull particle contact rejects empty collision-box space.
The beach, pier, night lanterns, powered boat and simulated
foam, bubbles and spray remain. Calm regions use Hamiltonian waves; complex
flow and body interactions activate 3D fluid regions without changing cell size.

Indoor labs start with a solid sinking glass ball. The ocean starts with a
lighter hollow ball. Settings can switch Sink/Float; reset preserves the choice.
Hold Space underwater to swim upward even in Sink mode.

Water launchers select Balanced DLSS; Neon Night selects Quality.
Automatic frame generation is selected where supported.
Both normal and fisheye lenses support FG. Settings offers DLSS RR Quality,
Balanced and Performance without resetting water. Render FPS and actual DLSS
output FPS are separate. Generated frames do not accelerate fluid simulation.
Release tests were skipped for 0.1.5. No fresh frame-generation output
verification was performed; the adjacent verification report records this.

Change lighting, flashlight, ball mode, music volume and water quality in Esc.
Path tracing defaults to fast RIS, 1 base path, 2 light samples, 4 imported-light
candidates, 4 surface bounces and Russian roulette. Esc exposes these settings.
Applying water quality resets the water. Filling stops at capacity.
Optional launchers also include Hamiltonian small water and ReSTIR PT lighting.

If startup fails, read NVMatrixEngine.log and lab-error.txt in this folder.
Keep the executable beside its shaders, assets, DLLs and D3D12 folder.
Verify the ZIP with its SHA-256 sidecar. The build is unsigned.
